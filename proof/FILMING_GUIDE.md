# Filming your room for Stash

Thanks for helping. This takes about five minutes, and the video itself is 60 to 90 seconds.

## What I'm asking
- **The video:** I'd like to use a short video of your bedroom to build a 3D copy of it for a small hide-and-seek game, entered in a 3D AI hackathon.
- **Where it's shown:** the game will be public online until at least the end of December 2026.
- **What you can do:**
  - Say no at any point.
  - Ask me to take the room down later.
  - Choose whether your name appears. You can use a first name, a handle, or nothing.

## Before you film
1. **Pick the room.** It's best if it has a dresser or chest of drawers, a wardrobe or closet door, and one or two bedside tables.
2. **Tidy anything private.** Clear away photos of people, documents, screens with messages, and anything with an address on it. The 3D copy shows everything the camera sees.
3. **Make it bright.** Turn on the main light and open the curtains.
4. **Close the drawers and doors.**

## Filming (iPhone)
1. Use the normal Camera app in **Video** mode, at 1x zoom. Don't use Cinematic or Portrait mode.
2. Hold the phone upright and stand in the doorway.
3. **Walk slowly** around the room. It should take 60 to 90 seconds.
4. Keep the camera pointed at the furniture, about one to two metres away, at chest height.
5. **Pass each piece of furniture twice:**
   - once straight on;
   - once from an angle, so the side shows too.
6. At the end, take one slow look at the floor and one at the ceiling corners.
7. Don't zoom, don't move fast, and don't film mirrors up close.

## Measurements (2 minutes, optional but very useful)
With a tape measure, write down:
- how far the top drawer of the dresser pulls out, in cm;
- how far one bedside drawer pulls out, in cm;
- roughly how wide the wardrobe door opens: a quarter turn, half, or almost flat.

These check the 3D joints against the real furniture.

## Sending it
- Send the original video file, not a compressed chat copy. AirDrop, Google Drive or iCloud link all work.
- In the same message, reply with:
  1. "Yes, you can use this video of my room in Stash."
  2. How you want to be credited: a first name, a handle, or none.
  3. The measurements, if you took them.

If anything in the room makes you uneasy, tell me and I'll cut that part or not use the video.
